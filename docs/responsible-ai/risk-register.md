# Risk register (S2-31)

**Owner:** Samukelo · **Last updated:** Mon 9 Nov 2026

Each risk says who could be harmed, how likely and serious it is, what Relay does about it today, and where to see that control working. **Likelihood** and **impact** are rated Low, Medium or High for Kopano Freight's current use (about 350 staff, with live requests). Ratings are the team's judgement and should be reviewed with Kopano Freight.

**Status:** *In place* = built and tested. *Partly* = some control built, more needed. *Open* = not yet handled.

| ID | Risk | Who could be harmed | Likelihood | Impact | What Relay does now | Where to see it | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A request is sent to the wrong department | The staff member waits longer; the wrong team does the work | Medium | Medium | Requests under 60% confidence are marked **Needs review**. An agent can move a request, and the move is recorded with the AI's original choice | Agent ticket view; Risk report, "Routings changed by an agent" and "Marked Needs review" | In place |
| R2 | The classifier routes by place name (depots) rather than by the problem | Depot staff get IT help more slowly | Medium | Low | Both misses from the bias test were marked Needs review. Fix planned for the next retrain: remove depot and place names before training and classifying | [bias-test.md](bias-test.md), section 2 | Partly |
| R3 | The classifier treats people differently by name | Staff from some groups get a worse service | Low | High | Bias test: 0 of 40 requests changed department across four names. Names can't be seen by the classifier at all | [bias-test.md](bias-test.md), section 1 | In place (tested on 40 requests) |
| R4 | Reply drafts differ in length or tone by name | Staff from some groups get less help or a less careful reply | Medium | Medium | Bias test on 50 drafts: the same tone, thanks and offered help for every name. Some single requests differ in length by up to 16 words; more drafts per name are planned | [bias-test.md](bias-test.md), section 3; [bias-test-drafts.md](bias-test-drafts.md) | Partly |
| R5 | A draft invents a name, amount, date or number | The staff member is told something untrue, or Kopano Freight makes a promise it can't keep | Medium | High | The prompt forbids invented details. After sending, the app checks every amount, date, time and number against the request, the template and earlier messages, and flags any it can't find | Flagged replies (`/compliance/flags`), "Possible invented detail"; Risk report | In place |
| R6 | An agent sends an AI draft without reading it | Replies may be wrong or careless | Medium | Medium | Every draft is labelled and editable. Replies that went out unchanged are flagged for review | Flagged replies, "Sent without edits"; Risk report, "Sent without edits" | Partly: a flag shows it happened, but doesn't stop it |
| R7 | Sensitive text (harassment, injury, health, wellbeing) is sent to an outside AI service | The staff member's private matter leaves Relay; a POPIA breach | Low | High | Sensitive requests are found by word rules before any AI call. Their text is never sent to Gemini; the draft starts from the Sensitive template | Agent ticket view ("Sensitive" tag); Risk report, "Sensitive requests kept from Gemini" | In place |
| R8 | A sensitive request is missed by the word rules | A harassment, safety or wellbeing report gets an everyday reply and is sent to Gemini | Medium | High | The word list is kept to clear phrases. Relay's rules can be extended, and each change is tested with a sample. Agents can still see the whole text and move the ticket | `src/lib/sensitive.ts`; sample tests recorded in the PR for each rule change | Partly: rules don't cover every wording (for example "shot", or slang) |
| R9 | A sensitive reply is sent unchanged from the template | The person gets an impersonal or wrong reply to a serious matter | Low | High | The reply box and the send action both block sending a sensitive reply without changes | Agent reply box; `sendReply` | In place |
| R10 | Gemini is slow, down or over its daily limit | Agents wait, or get a template that doesn't fit | High | Low | After 10 seconds, or on error, the department template is used and labelled as not AI. The Risk report warns when this happens often | Risk report, "AI unavailable" warning (migration 0016) | In place |
| R11 | Personal information leaves South Africa with Gemini | POPIA transfer and operator obligations | Medium | High | Sensitive requests stay in Relay. Non-sensitive requests (which may contain names and depots) do go to Gemini | [popia-note.md](popia-note.md), section 3 | Open: Kopano Freight must decide |
| R12 | Prompt injection: text in a request tells the AI to do something else | A draft says something the team didn't approve | Low | Medium | Gemini only returns text. It can't send, change data or call other services. A person reads and edits every draft; invented details are flagged | Agent reply box; flags | Partly |
| R13 | Someone changes a template and every later draft is wrong | Many replies go out wrong | Low | Medium | Only agents (their department) and managers can edit templates; each template records who last changed it | Template library (`updated_by`) | Partly: no approval step or change history yet |
| R14 | The AI's output is not labelled | People don't know they're reading machine text | Low | Medium | Every AI output carries its label. The Risk report checks each screen | Risk report, "AI labels on each screen" | Partly: staff "Sent to IT" screen and the dashboard insights and forecast aren't labelled yet |
| R15 | Staff see other people's requests | Privacy breach | Low | High | Row-level security on every table. Staff see only their own tickets; agents only their department's | [security.md](../security.md); migrations 0003 to 0004 | In place |
| R16 | Someone manages accounts they shouldn't (adds, changes or removes people) | Unauthorised access to requests | Low | High | Rules are enforced in the database. Nobody changes their own role or removes themselves; there is always a general manager; removed people can't sign in | [security.md](../security.md); `set_user_role()`, `deactivate_user()`, `can_manage_user()` | In place |
| R17 | Someone edits the audit log to hide what happened | Loss of accountability | Low | High | The audit log is append-only: nobody can update or delete entries. Entries are written in the signed-in person's name | Audit log (`/compliance/audit`); [audit-actions.md](../audit-actions.md) | In place |
| R18 | Sign-in is guessed or taken over | Unauthorised access | Medium | High | No public sign-up; invites expire; passwords at least 8 characters; forgot-password doesn't reveal accounts | [security.md](../security.md) | Partly: MFA not yet required for managers and compliance |
| R19 | The routing endpoint (`/api/classify`) is used by anyone | Misuse, or overload | Low | Low | It returns a department and a score for any text, with no sign-in and no rate limit | Known limit in [security.md](../security.md) and the architecture | Open: needs sign-in or a rate limit |
| R20 | A security incident (lost device, hacked account, phishing) is not reported in time | Personal data may be exposed; POPIA requires breach reporting | Medium | High | Security requests are marked Sensitive and routed to IT as high priority, with a note to tell a manager or compliance | Agent ticket view ("Sensitive: security") | Partly: no breach procedure written yet |
| R21 | Relay's AI reports are wrong without anyone noticing | Decisions made on false numbers | Low | Medium | Metrics are counted in the database, not by the AI. The Risk report shows the source counts | Risk report; `risk_metrics()` (migration 0014) | In place |
| R22 | The data kept by Relay is kept too long | POPIA: data should not be kept longer than needed | Medium | Medium | None yet. There is no agreed retention period | [popia-note.md](popia-note.md), section 5 | Open: Kopano Freight must decide |

## Review

- Review this register at each sprint review, and after any change to the classifier, the prompt, the sensitive rules or the flag checks.
- Open risks (R11, R19, R22) need a decision or a story before Sprint 4.
- A new risk gets a new row with the next ID, and the screen or check that handles it.
