# User guide

For people using Relay at Kopano Freight. Each section covers one role. Find your section by the role your manager gave you.

Relay has no sign-up page. Your manager adds you on **Manage users**. You get an invite email, choose a password, and then sign in with your work email. Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, and Relay emails you a link to choose a new one.

Relay can hold more than one company. You only see your own company's people, requests and reports.

## Staff: send and follow a request

**Send a request.** Go to **Submit request**. Write a subject and describe what you need. Choose your depot and a priority. Relay sends the request to the team that can answer it and shows you a reference number. Keep the reference if you phone the office.

**Waiting for approval.** Some requests need a manager's approval before a team starts on them: all leave, and Finance purchases over R5,000. The confirmation says **Waiting for approval**, and the request shows that status until a manager decides. If the manager declines, the reason is shown on the request.

**Follow a request.** Go to **My requests**. The newest is at the top. Each request shows its status in words and colour, and its request page shows the history and the reply once it is sent.

**Close or reopen.** When a request is answered, you can:
- **This solved it**: closes the request.
- **I still need help**: sends a message back to the team and reopens it for them.

You only see your own requests.

## Agent: answer requests in your department

**Inbox.** Your inbox has your department's requests, most urgent first. Use the filters to show **Mine** (what you have taken) or **Assigned to**. Requests marked **Needs review** were routed with low confidence, so check the department first.

**Take a request.** Click **Take ticket**. You can take any request in your department, except your own. If you sent the request, another agent in your department takes it.

**Check the department.** If a request is in the wrong department, use the option to move it to another department. Use **Department is correct** when you have checked it. Moving a request is recorded in the audit log.

**Reply.** Relay writes an AI draft from the template for the department. It is labelled **AI-drafted**. Choose a tone (formal, friendly or urgent), then edit the draft as you see fit, and send it. The original draft is kept with your sent text.

- Only the agent who took the request can send the reply.
- You can't send while the request is **Waiting for approval**.
- A sensitive request (for example, harassment, self-harm, an injury or a security breach) is not sent to the AI. It uses the department's **Sensitive** template, and its reply must be changed before it can be sent.

**Templates.** The template library holds the reply templates for your department. Edit them there.

## Manager: run your department

Your menu depends on whether you manage one department or all of them.

- **Department manager** (for example, HR): you see your own department only.
- **General manager** (no department): you see and act on every department.

Your menu also has **Tickets** (the department's inbox, as for agents), **Submit request** and **My requests**, so you can send your own requests too.

**Dashboard.** Requests, response times and the weekly summary. Department managers see their own department only.

**Approvals.** Purchases and leave waiting for a decision, oldest first. Approve, or decline with a reason. You can't decide your own request, so it isn't listed. Another manager decides it.

**Manage users.** Add people, change roles, edit names, emails and depots, and remove people who have left. Department managers can add and change agents in their own department only. A general manager adds staff, managers and compliance officers. A removed person keeps their history.

**Company settings.** On **Manage users**, a general manager sets the company's depots and the email domains people can be invited with. Leave the domains empty to allow any domain. Save with **Save settings**.

**Forecast.** The expected number of requests for the next 28 days, with a range. It is a guide to typical volume.

**Reports.** Weekly reports for a department, with a PDF download. Department managers see their own department's reports.

**Two-step sign-in.** You also need a six-digit code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator). The first time you sign in, Relay asks you to set it up. After that, it asks for a code at each sign-in. If you lose your phone, ask Samukelo to reset it.

## Compliance: check the AI and the audit trail

**Audit log.** Every action taken in Relay: routing, assignments, replies sent, approvals and user changes.

**Flagged replies.** AI replies marked for a second look, either automatically or by a person. Review each one and resolve it: OK, or needs change, with a note.

**Risk report.** The numbers behind Relay's responsible-AI checks, for the whole company.

**Two-step sign-in.** As for managers, you need a code from your authenticator app at each sign-in.

## Getting help

- If something on the screen doesn't match this guide, note the reference number and tell your manager.
- Your manager can put you in touch with the Relay team.

## Platform admin: set up a company

This section is for Relay's own platform team, not for company staff. A platform admin:

- sees every company and its counts on the **Companies** page (`/system`),
- creates a company with its name, a short address name and the name and email of its **system admin**. The system admin gets an invite email and becomes that company's general manager,
- opens a company to see its people in tabs (Staff, Agents, Managers, Company admins and Compliance), and to **invite another system admin** into that company,
- can edit a person's name, department, depot and email, but can't see the company's tickets or replies.

Platform admins use two-step sign-in, as set for their company.
